How credit card interest works

If you don’t pay the full bill by the due date, the card charges interest on what you owe — typically 3% to 3.75% a month in India, which is 36% to 45% a year. In India, 18% GST is added to that interest. Each payment first covers these charges; only the rest reduces the balance.

The months to clear a balance B with a fixed payment P at monthly rate r are n = −ln(1 − r·B ÷ P) ÷ ln(1 + r). If P is not larger than r·B, the balance never falls.

Ways to pay less interest

  • Pay more than the minimum — even a little more cuts years off.
  • Stop using the card until it is cleared; new purchases lose the interest-free period while a balance is carried.
  • Ask your bank to convert the balance to EMIs, or use a lower-rate personal loan, and compare the total cost here.
  • Pay as early in the cycle as you can, since interest is charged daily.

FAQ

Questions about Credit Card Payoff Calculator

Where does Credit Card Payoff Calculator run?

Credit Card Payoff Calculator runs in this tab on Toolspire. What you type or drop in is not sent to us and is not stored on a server.

How long will it take to pay off my credit card?

It depends on the balance, the rate and your payment. For ₹1,00,000 at 3.5% a month with GST, ₹10,000 a month takes 14 months; ₹5,000 a month takes 3 years 8 months. Enter your own numbers above.

Why is paying only the minimum due a bad idea?

The minimum is usually 5% of what you owe, barely more than the monthly interest and GST, so the balance hardly falls — and as it falls, the minimum falls too. A ₹1,00,000 balance can take decades and cost several times the original amount.

Is GST charged on credit card interest?

In India, yes: 18% GST applies to finance charges, late fees and other card fees. A 3.5% monthly rate therefore costs about 4.13% a month in practice.

What is the difference between the monthly rate and APR?

APR is the yearly rate: the monthly rate × 12. A card charging 3.5% a month has an APR of 42%. Switch the rate between “per month” and “per year” to use whichever your statement shows.

Does this include new purchases?

No. It assumes you stop adding to the balance. If you keep spending on the card, payoff takes longer.

Is my data stored?

No. The calculation runs in this tab.
