**How many cm is 5 feet 10 inches?**
5 ft 10 in is 177.8 cm. Type “5 ft 10 in to cm” in the box at the top to convert any height.

**How do I convert Celsius to Fahrenheit?**
Multiply by 9/5 and add 32: 37 °C × 9/5 + 32 = 98.6 °F. To go back, subtract 32 and multiply by 5/9.

**How many square feet are in a guntha, acre or bigha?**
A guntha is 1,089 sq ft and an acre is 43,560 sq ft (40 guntha). A bigha differs by state; in West Bengal and Assam it is 14,400 sq ft.

**What is the difference between GB and GiB?**
A gigabyte (GB) is 1,000,000,000 bytes; a gibibyte (GiB) is 1,073,741,824 bytes (1024³). Drive makers use GB, while Windows shows sizes in GiB but labels them “GB” — which is why a 1 TB drive appears as about 931 GB.

**How do I convert lakh and crore to million?**
1 lakh = 100,000 and 1 crore = 10 million, so 10 lakh = 1 million and 100 crore = 1 billion. Choose the “Lakh & crore” category.

**What does an AC “ton” mean?**
It measures cooling power: 1 ton of refrigeration = 12,000 BTU per hour ≈ 3.517 kW of cooling. A 1.5 ton AC removes about 5.3 kW of heat — it is not its electricity use.

### FAQ

**Where does Unix Timestamp Converter run?**
Unix Timestamp Converter runs in this tab on Toolspire. What you type or drop in is not sent to us and is not stored on a server.

**What is Unix time (epoch time)?**
The number of seconds since 00:00:00 UTC on 1 January 1970, not counting leap seconds. It is the same everywhere in the world; time zones only change how it is displayed.

**Is my timestamp in seconds or milliseconds?**
Count the digits: today’s times have 10 digits in seconds and 13 in milliseconds (16 in microseconds, 19 in nanoseconds). The converter detects this automatically.

**What is the Year 2038 problem?**
Systems that store Unix time as a signed 32-bit number overflow after 2,147,483,647 — 03:14:07 UTC on 19 January 2038. Modern systems use 64-bit time, which lasts billions of years.

**How do I convert an Excel date to a timestamp?**
Choose “Excel / Sheets serial date” as the format and paste the number. Excel counts days from 30 December 1899, so 45292 is 1 January 2024.

**Does Unix time change with daylight saving?**
No. Unix time always counts UTC seconds. Daylight saving only changes the local time shown for it — which the converter applies for the zone you choose.

#### Why sizes differ — and what to rely on

Bigha, biswa and katha are older than any national standard, and each region kept its own. Many places even have two: a larger **pucca** bigha used in records and a smaller **kaccha** bigha used in everyday talk.

Government records are kept in **hectares** (or acres), and sale deeds are registered in square metres or hectares. For a purchase, loan or mutation, take the area from the record of rights — khatauni, jamabandi, 7/12 extract or patta — and confirm the local unit with the lekhpal, patwari or tehsil office. Then type that size here so the conversion matches.

#### Formulas

- Plot area in sq ft = length (ft) × width (ft).
- Gaj (sq yd) = sq ft ÷ 9.
- Square metres = sq ft ÷ 10.7639.
- Acres = sq ft ÷ 43,560. Hectares = sq ft ÷ 1,07,639.
- Bigha = sq ft ÷ the bigha of your state — for example ÷ 27,000 in Uttar Pradesh or ÷ 14,400 in West Bengal.
- Rate per bigha = rate per sq ft × sq ft in a bigha.
