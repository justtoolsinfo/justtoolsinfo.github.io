### Guide

#### How the deductions are calculated

| Deduction | Rule used |
| --- | --- |
| Provident fund (PF) | 12% of Basic + DA; with the wage ceiling, on at most ₹15,000 (₹1,800 a month) |
| Employer PF | Another 12%: 8.33% to the pension scheme (on up to ₹15,000) and the rest to PF |

When an employee is paid for fewer days than the month has, each earning is reduced in proportion: **earned = monthly amount × paid days ÷ days in the month**. For example, ₹45,000 Basic with 27 of 30 days paid gives ₹40,500. PF and ESI are then worked out on the reduced pay.

### Guide

#### What a Luxembourg invoice must show

Article 63 §8 of the VAT law lists the details; the company law of 10 August 1915 adds the identification of companies. This tool prints all of them:

- The **date of issue** and a **unique sequential number**, which may follow one or more series.
- Your **VAT number**, and the **customer’s VAT number** when they account for the VAT (reverse charge) or receive an intra-Community supply of goods.
- The **full name and address** of both parties.
- The **quantity and nature** of the goods or the **extent and nature** of the services, and the **date of supply** if it differs from the invoice date.
- The **taxable amount for each rate** or exemption, the **unit price excluding VAT**, discounts, the **rate** and the **VAT due**.
- For an exemption, the **legal reference**; for reverse charge, **“Autoliquidation”**; with cash accounting, **“Comptabilité de caisse”**.
- For a company: its name with the **legal form** (SA, SARL…), the **registered office** and **“R.C.S. Luxembourg”** with its registration number.

#### Luxembourg VAT rates

Which rate applies depends on the annexes of the VAT law and its regulations — check with the AED (Administration de l’enregistrement, des domaines et de la TVA) if you are not sure for a given product.

| Rate | Name | Typical uses |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 17 % | Normal rate | Most goods and services |
| 14 % | Intermediate rate | Wines up to 13 % alcohol, mineral fuels and heating oil, washing and cleaning products, advertising print, custody of securities (annex C) |
| 8 % | Reduced rate | Gas, electricity, district heating, firewood, hairdressing, repairs of shoes, clothes and household appliances, bicycles (annex A) |
| 3 % | Super-reduced rate | Food (not alcoholic drinks), books and newspapers, children’s clothes, medicines, restaurant meals (not alcohol), hotels, passenger transport, entry to culture and sport (annex B) |

#### Small businesses: the VAT franchise

Businesses established in Luxembourg whose annual turnover in Luxembourg does not exceed **€50,000** (the threshold since 1 January 2025) can use the franchise: they charge no VAT and cannot deduct it. If turnover passes €50,000 the exemption continues until the end of the year as long as it stays within €55,000; above that it ends the next day (AED SME scheme FAQ).

Franchise invoices may not show VAT and must carry the statement “TVA non applicable – Article 57bis de la loi modifiée du 12 février 1979”. Since 2025 they may also be simplified invoices.

#### E-invoicing in Luxembourg

Invoices to Luxembourg **public bodies** must be electronic, in the European standard EN 16931 sent over Peppol — for all suppliers since 18 March 2023. The **e-invoice (UBL)** button makes such a file; send it through a Peppol access point or the State’s e-invoicing service.

For **business-to-business** invoices, a government bill (n° 8815, filed in July 2026) proposes compulsory e-invoicing from 2028 for receiving and from 2028–2029 for issuing. It is still a draft; until it becomes law, PDF invoices remain fine between businesses.

1. **Paste 11 characters** — The sample should read Valid format.
2. **Change the fifth character** — It should fail.
3. **Confirm the branch elsewhere** — A passed format can still be an unused code.

### Guide

#### Eleven characters and a reserved zero

The published IFSC shape is four letters, then a zero, then six alphanumeric characters. SBIN0001234 fits. SBIN1001234 does not, even though it is the same length. The page checks that pattern and nothing else. It does not know which bank owns the first four letters, and it does not know whether the branch code was issued.

### FAQ

**Where does IFSC Format Checker run?**
IFSC Format Checker runs in this tab on Toolspire. What you type or drop in is not sent to us and is not stored on a server.

**Does SBIN0001234 fit the format?**
Yes.

**Why does SBIN1001234 fail?**
The fifth character must be 0. It is 1.

**Does a valid format mean the branch exists?**
No.

**Are lowercase letters allowed?**
They are uppercased first. The shape is then checked.
