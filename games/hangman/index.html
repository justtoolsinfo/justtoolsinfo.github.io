The sequence

How to play Hangman

  1. 1

    Choose a category

    Pick a category and a level. The word appears as a row of blanks, one per letter.

  2. 2

    Guess a letter

    Tap or type a letter. If it’s in the word, it fills in everywhere it appears.

  3. 3

    Watch the drawing

    A wrong letter adds a part to the drawing and costs one life.

  4. 4

    Solve it

    Fill in every letter, or type the whole word if you know it, before your lives run out.

  5. 5

    Play again

    Press Next word (or Enter) for a new word — your streak carries on while you keep winning.

Hangman tips

  • Start with vowels — E, A, O and I appear in most words.
  • Then try common consonants such as T, N, S, R and L. English letter frequency runs roughly E, T, A, O, I, N, S, H, R.
  • Look at the length and the gaps. A word ending in _ _ _ might be -ING or -ION.
  • Use the category. In Countries, think of endings like -IA and -LAND.
  • Save the whole-word guess until you’re fairly sure — a wrong one costs a life.

About Hangman

Hangman is a guessing game for two or more players, usually played with pencil and paper: one player thinks of a word and draws a blank for each letter, and the others guess letters until they solve it or the drawing is finished. Its origins are unclear, but Alice Gomme’s Traditional Games of England, Scotland, and Ireland (1894) describes a version called “Birds, Beasts and Fishes”.

FAQ

Questions about Hangman

Where does Hangman run?

Hangman runs in this tab on Toolspire. What you type or drop in is not sent to us and is not stored on a server.

How do you play Hangman?

Guess the hidden word one letter at a time. Right letters fill in the blanks; each wrong letter adds a part to the drawing. Solve the word before the drawing is finished.

How many wrong guesses do you get?

Six on Normal, which is the usual number on paper (head, body, two arms, two legs). Easy gives 10 and Hard gives 4.

Can two people play Hangman here?

Yes — choose Two players. One person types a secret word (with an optional clue) while the other looks away, then the other guesses.

Is there a version for kids?

Yes. Pick Easy and the Balloons picture: a balloon pops with each miss instead of the classic drawing.

What are good letters to guess first?

Vowels — E, A, O, I — then common consonants such as T, N, S, R and L.

Is it free?

Yes, free with no account. Your settings and record are saved only in this browser.
