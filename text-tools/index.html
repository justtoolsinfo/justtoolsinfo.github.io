FAQ

Text Tools: common questions

What belongs in Text Tools on Toolspire?

Text Tools is one collection on justtools.info. Open any tool and the page tells you whether the work stays on your device or a public lookup is required.

Is my text sent to a server?

No. Text tools on Toolspire work on your text inside your browser, and nothing you type or paste is sent to us or logged. The one choice that involves another service is yours: the teleprompter’s voice following can use your browser’s online speech recognition, and asks first.

Do the text tools support Hindi and other languages?

Yes. Counting is Unicode-aware, so Hindi and other Indic scripts, Chinese, Japanese, Thai and emoji are all handled correctly.

Can I use these tools on my phone?

Yes. Every tool is responsive and works in any modern mobile browser with no app to install.
