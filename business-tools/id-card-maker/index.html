Printing on paper

  • Print at 100% / Actual size. “Fit to page” shrinks the cards slightly, so they no longer fit standard holders.
  • For double-sided cards, print the front sheet, then the back sheet on the other side. Most printers flip on the long edge; if your backs come out upside down, choose “flip on short edge” instead.
  • Thick paper or card stock (250–300 gsm) gives the best result; then laminate the cards and cut along the marks.
  • Do a test on plain paper first and hold it up to the light to check that fronts and backs line up.

Printing on PVC cards

Direct-to-card PVC printers print through a printer driver set to the card size. Choose Card printer (one per page): the PDF then has one card-sized page per side — front, back, next front — which the driver prints in order (set it to double-sided for backs).

Card printers cannot print right to the edge on every model; keep important text a few millimetres in from the border, as the layouts here already do.

Bulk cards from a spreadsheet

Put one person per row with a heading row on top: Name, the line under the name (Designation, Class…), each field label and Photo. Download the sample CSV to see the exact headings for your template. Headings such as Dept, DOB, Mobile or Blood are understood too.

Name each photo after the person’s card number (EMP-1042.jpg) or name (Priya Sharma.jpg), or type its file name in the Photo column, then add all the photos at once.

FAQ

Questions about Printable ID Card Maker

Where does Printable ID Card Maker run?

Printable ID Card Maker runs in this tab on Toolspire. What you type or drop in is not sent to us and is not stored on a server.

What size is a standard ID card?

85.6 × 54 mm (3.375 × 2.125 in) — the ISO/IEC 7810 ID-1 size, also called CR80, the same as a credit card. It has rounded corners of about 3.2 mm.

How many ID cards fit on an A4 sheet?

Ten landscape cards (2 × 5) or nine portrait cards (3 × 3), leaving a 6 mm margin that most printers need. US Letter takes eight landscape or nine portrait cards.

Can I make ID cards for a whole class or company at once?

Yes. Paste rows from Excel or Google Sheets, or open a CSV, and add the photos. Up to 500 people go into one print PDF.

What can the QR code contain?

The card number, a link or text with placeholders such as https://example.com/verify/{id}, or a vCard contact with name, organisation, title, phone and website. A barcode (Code 128) can hold the card number or short text.

Will it work with my PVC card printer?

Choose “Card printer (one per page)”: every page is exactly card size, which is what card printer drivers expect. Print a single test card first to check alignment.

Are the photos and details uploaded anywhere?

No. The cards are made in this tab. The design and the single card you edit are saved on this device so they are there next time; imported lists and photos are kept only while the tab is open.

Can I put a signature on the card?

Yes, on the back: upload a photo of the signature (on white paper, cropped close) and set the signatory’s title.

Does it support Hindi names?

Yes — names and fields in Devanagari are drawn with a font that covers it, with the vowel signs in the right places.
